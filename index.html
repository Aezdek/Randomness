    3. 03

    The checklist

    • Boots cleanly, no error icons
    • Full capacity shows in Settings › About
    • Storage scanned for errors
    • Holds charge through a run-down test
    • Charges from USB and a wall adapter
    • Syncs with Mac (Finder) and Windows (iTunes)
    • Wheel scrolls smoothly, no dead spots
    • Menu, play, back, forward and centre buttons
    • Hold switch locks and unlocks
    • Headphone jack: both channels, no crackle when wiggled
    • Screen: no dead pixels, even backlight
    • Dock connector clean and seated

    Questions

    Will it work with my computer?

    Yes. On a Mac it shows up in Finder (macOS Catalina and later), or in iTunes on older Macs. On Windows, use iTunes. Every iPod is sync-tested on both before it's listed.

    Does it come with music on it?

    No. Every iPod is wiped and restored to factory settings, so it arrives empty and ready for your own library.

    What's a flash or SD upgrade?

    iPod classics originally used tiny spinning hard drives, which are now the number one thing that fails. Swapping the drive for SD-card flash storage makes the iPod silent, lighter, much more shock-proof and often far bigger. It also frees up room inside for a larger battery.

    How long does the battery last?

    Every iPod either gets a new battery or passes a run-down test before it's listed, and the listing says which. Upgraded classics with high-capacity batteries can go several days of normal listening between charges.

    What if something goes wrong?

    Every iPod comes with a 30-day warranty. If it develops a fault, message me and I'll repair it, replace it or refund you.

    How do payment and shipping work?

    If an iPod is also listed on eBay you can buy it there with eBay's buyer protection. For direct orders I send a secure invoice, then ship it tracked, padded and boxed within two business days.
